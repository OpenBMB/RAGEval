### **Industry Overview**
The global renewable energy industry is experiencing rapid growth, driven by the increasing need for clean and sustainable energy sources. Governments worldwide are implementing policies to incentivize the adoption of renewable energy, while consumers and businesses are recognizing the financial and environmental benefits of transitioning to renewable energy solutions. Solar, wind, and energy storage systems are expected to see significant growth, as prices for renewable energy technologies continue to decline and their efficiency improves.

Our target market includes:
- **Residential Customers**: Homeowners seeking to reduce energy costs and carbon footprints by adopting solar panels and energy storage systems.

- **Industrial Customers**: Large-scale energy consumers, including factories and manufacturing plants, that need reliable and cost-effective renewable energy systems to power their operations.

By understanding the pain points and energy needs of these customer segments, Renewable Energy Example Company is able to provide tailored solutions that meet both functional and financial requirements.
