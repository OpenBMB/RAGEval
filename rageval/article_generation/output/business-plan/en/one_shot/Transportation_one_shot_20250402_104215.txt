**Executive Summary:**

RoadRunner Transport is a newly established transportation company based in Dallas, Texas, specializing in providing premium transportation services for businesses and individuals. The company's mission is to offer reliable, efficient, and safe transportation solutions to meet the diverse needs of our clients.

RoadRunner Transport's primary focus will be on offering a range of transportation services, including airport transfers, corporate transportation, event transportation, and city tours. With a commitment to exceptional customer service and leveraging advanced technology, we aim to establish ourselves as a leading transportation provider in the Dallas metropolitan area.

Key Objectives:

1. Achieve a 10% market share in the first year of operation.
2. Expand our service offerings to include courier services within the second year.
3. Increase market share by a minimum of 15% annually for the first three years.
   
**Company Summary:**

RoadRunner Transport's fleet will consist of a variety of vehicles, including sedans, SUVs, vans, and luxury vehicles, to cater to different client preferences and group sizes. The company will operate from a strategically located facility in Dallas with easy access to major highways and the airport.

Start-up Requirements:

Total funding required: $800,000
Start-up expenses: $50,000
Start-up assets: $750,000
   
**Services:**

RoadRunner Transport will offer the following transportation services:

- Airport transfers to and from major airports in the Dallas-Fort Worth area.
- Corporate transportation for business clients, including executive car services and shuttle services for employees.
- Event transportation for weddings, corporate events, and social gatherings.
- City tours for tourists and locals to explore the attractions of Dallas.
   
**Market Analysis:**

Research indicates a strong demand for reliable transportation services in the Dallas metropolitan area, driven by the city's growing economy and increasing tourism. The target market includes corporate clients, tourists, event planners, and individuals seeking premium transportation options.

Within a 20-mile radius of our location:

- Population exceeds 2 million
- Median age is 32
- Average household size is 3.2 persons
- Average household income is $75,000
   
**Strategy and Implementation:**

RoadRunner Transport's competitive edge will be derived from:

- Offering a diverse range of transportation services to meet various client needs.
- Investing in advanced technology for online booking, GPS tracking, and customer communication.
- Recruiting experienced drivers with a focus on safety, professionalism, and customer service.
- Developing partnerships with hotels, event planners, and corporations to secure recurring business.
- Implementing a targeted marketing strategy to raise brand awareness and attract customers.

The projected average revenue per customer is estimated at $50 per trip, with an anticipated annual revenue of $1.2 million in the first year, growing to $2.5 million by the third year.
